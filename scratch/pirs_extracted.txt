1. AUDIT PREPARATION & LIAISON 
Objective:
Ensure a smooth and compliant audit process for the previous
fiscal year (ending July 31 2025), including liaisoning with the
auditor.
Scope of Work:
Act as primary liaison with external auditor
Prepare financial statements and documentation for FY audit,
alongside outgoing Finance Director
Conduct a deep-dive review of prior year financials, including
time with the Finance Director to clarify key entries and resolve
outstanding questions
Review and reconcile entries and resolve discrepancies
Coordinate with bookkeeper to compile and validate backup
documentation
Respond to auditor questions within 1–2 business days
Join auditor meetings and coordinate all follow-up items
Prepare PBC (Prepared By Client) list items and ensure timely
delivery
Provide summary audit report and handover notes post-audit
Note: It is ideal to maintain access to the outgoing Finance Director
for 4 weeks for audit preparation purposes. 
Timeline: Approx 8-10 weeks - based on audit timeline

============================== PAGE 3 ==============================
2. BOOKKEEPING OVERSIGHT &
FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT
Objective:
Ensure accurate, timely, and funder-compliant bookkeeping through
hands-on oversight, quality control, and support for PIRS’s internal
bookkeeper, while gradually introducing process improvements and
clearer financial reporting systems.
Scope of Work:
Manage all AP/AR and monthly reconciliations to ensure accuracy
Oversee day-to-day tasks of junior bookkeeper with regular
check-ins and support especially for accounts payable
Evaluate bookkeeper’s performance over a 2–3 month period and
recommend future staffing model
Manage accounts receivable, including invoicing, grant
management and donations
Develop and implement GL coding standards aligned with
internal, funder, audit requirements
Conduct monthly financial reviews and trial balance checks for
accuracy
Assist with cash flow tracking and monthly budget variance
analysis tied to project-based reporting needs
Prepare monthly financial summary reports for EDs and
leadership
Develop a bookkeeping calendar and approval workflow 
Timeline: Ongoing

============================== PAGE 4 ==============================
3. PAYROLL MANAGEMENT 
Objective:
To manage end-to-end payroll processing and accounting w/ system
integration and compliance, while evaluating platform options. 
Scope of Work:
Manage payroll cycles (weekly, bi-weekly, semi-monthly, or
monthly) for full-time, part-time, seasonal, and contract staff
Process hourly staff and contractor payments, including
government-funded programs like Canada Summer Jobs
Handle employee changes, new hires, terminations, and vacation
tracking within payroll software
Reconcile payroll data with QuickBooks each cycle; implement
workarounds to reduce manual spreadsheet use
Ensure accurate source deductions (CPP, EI, income tax) and
submit federal/provincial remittances
Track payroll liabilities such as vacation pay, statutory
deductions, and year-end accruals
Prepare and file T4, T4A, and ROE forms in line with CRA and
provincial requirements
Maintain and update employee records in payroll system
Develop a payroll calendar and approval workflow with manager
cutoffs and review steps
Evaluate whether to continue with PayWorks or transition to
QBO Payroll for better integration and efficiency
Note: While we manage all payroll accounting and processing,
managers must provide timely payroll data (e.g., timesheets, new
hires, exits). We do not manage HR functions.
Timeline: Ongoing

4. DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION &
STAFF TRAINING
Objective:
Conduct a full digital systems audit to identify integration gaps,
inefficiencies, and implement inclusive, user-friendly processes that
improve funder reporting and reduce manual work.
Scope of Work:
1. Audit & Strategy Development
Conduct a deep-dive review of current financial systems,
processes, and overall tech stack
Hold meetings with the outgoing Finance Director and current
team to understand existing workflows, strengths, and
challenges
Assess the current chart of accounts, class structure, and GL
setup to identify gaps and areas for improvement
Evaluate existing budget templates at both the organizational
and departmental levels
Review project and funder reporting templates and compare
them to internal financial reporting tools
Map current workflows and process flows; identify inefficiencies,
redundancies, and compliance risks
Assess digital literacy levels across staff and review system
integration points between teams
Recommend updated workflows, approval processes, and
internal controls to improve efficiency and compliance
Propose refinements to the tech stack to reduce manual work
and improve automation and reporting
Develop and deliver future-state process maps and a clear
transformation roadmap
